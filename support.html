Support

Help with alarms, family linking, Premium and your data.

My alarm did not ring

Open Settings → Alarm reliability in Medify and turn on every item it lists. On Android this includes exact alarms, notifications, and removing battery optimisation for Medify. Phones from Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo, Vivo and OnePlus also need Autostart turned on; Medify takes you to the right screen where it can. A linked family member sees a warning if your phone may not ring reliably.

How do I link a family member?

On the patient's phone, open Family → Invite a family member and share the 6-digit code. On the family member's phone, choose Join as caregiver and enter it. Codes work once and expire after 24 hours. It is easiest during a visit or a video call.

How do I stop sharing?

On the patient's phone, open Family, choose the person and tap Stop sharing. It takes effect immediately and does not need their approval. Your medicines and reminders stay as they are.

Premium

To restore a purchase, open Settings → Premium → Restore purchase with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. To cancel, use your App Store or Google Play subscriptions page.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account. Your data is removed within 7 days. If a family member is linked, they are told first. See the Privacy Policy for details.
