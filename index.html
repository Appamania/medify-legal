Reminds the patient. Reassures the family.

Medify rings at the right time for every medicine. One tap logs it: Taken, Skipped or Snooze. A family member you link sees how it is going, and hears from Medify only when a pattern of missed doses means something is wrong.

What's inside

  • Alarms that ring on time, even with no signal. Unlimited medicines, free.
  • Taken, Skipped or Snooze 15 min, in one tap from the alarm.
  • Link a family member with a 6-digit code. Stop sharing any time, instantly.
  • Quiet by default: family is alerted when a critical medicine is skipped, when the same medicine is skipped 3 times in a week, or when 5 doses are skipped in a week.
  • Refill reminders before you run out.
  • English, हिन्दी and বাংলা. A larger, easier-to-read font if you want it.

Free for one person

Every reminder and every alert is free for one patient. Medify Premium (₹99 a month or ₹699 a year) is for family members looking after more than one person, and adds doctor-ready PDF reports.

Medify helps you remember. It does not replace your doctor's advice. It does not diagnose, suggest doses or check drug interactions.
