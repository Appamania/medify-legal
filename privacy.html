Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Medify is a medicine-reminder app made by Appamania ("we"). Because Medify stores information about the medicines you take, we treat everything in it as health data. This policy explains what we store, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary for this data.

In short: we store only what you add to the app, we keep it in India, we never sell it or use it for advertising, a family member sees it only if you choose to share, and you can stop sharing or delete everything at any time.

Medicine names, form, dose, schedule, whether a medicine is marked critical, and pill countsTo ring reminders at the right time and remind you to refill.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, messages or microphone, and nothing from other apps. No advertising identifiers. Medify shows no ads.

Your consent

Medify asks for two separate consents when you start. The first, which is required, lets us store your medicine information so we can remind you. The second, which is optional, lets a family member you link see it. You can use Medify fully without the second. You can withdraw either consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & data. Withdrawing sharing takes effect immediately. Withdrawing the first means deleting your account, as described below.

Sharing with family

If you link a family member, they can see your medicine names and times, whether each dose was taken or skipped, reasons you give for skipping, and whether your phone can ring alarms. They can never see your location, messages, other apps, or anything outside Medify. They cannot change your medicines. You can stop sharing at any time without their approval.

Where your data is stored, and who processes it

Your account and medicine data are stored in Google Cloud's Mumbai region (India) using Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. These service providers process data on our behalf:

ProviderWhat it receivesWhy
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Remote Config)The data described aboveTo run your account, store and sync your data, detect missed-dose patterns, send alerts, and fix crashes.
An Indian SMS provider, registered under TRAI's DLT rulesYour emergency contact's phone number and the alert textTo send the critical-dose SMS. Only used if you set an emergency contact and skip a critical medicine.
Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCatSubscription status and an anonymous purchase identifierTo deliver and restore Medify Premium. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google; we never see them.

We do not sell personal data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

How long data is kept

  • Dose history is kept in full for 12 months. After that it is reduced to monthly percentages taken, and the individual entries are deleted.
  • When you delete your account, your data is deleted within 7 days. If a family member is linked, they are told first and the 7 days give them time to save a report.
  • A linked family member's own notes and acknowledgements stay in their account, with your identity removed.
  • Crash reports are kept by Google for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit or delete medicines, stop sharing in Family, or delete everything in Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account. For anything else, write to our Grievance Officer below. You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Adults only

Medify is for people aged 18 or older. You confirm your age when you start. We do not knowingly process data of children. If you believe a child is using Medify, contact us and we will delete the account.

Not medical advice

Medify helps you remember. It does not diagnose, suggest doses, or check drug interactions, and it does not replace your doctor's advice.

Security and breaches

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by your account and by database rules: a family member can only read, never change, what you share. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change affects what we do with your data, we will tell you in the app and ask again where your consent is needed.
